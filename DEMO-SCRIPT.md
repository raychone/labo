# Demo script

1. Log in with `manager@demo.local`.
2. Open Panou principal and show the authenticated shell.
3. Open Lucrări.
4. Search `Maria Dumitrescu`.
5. Filter by `Clinica Dentară Aurora`.
6. Filter by `Dr. Ana Popescu`.
7. Open a work and show the QR panel.
8. Open Billing.
9. Select the current month.
10. Show uninvoiced works.
11. Search `PF-<year>-000001` and show the issued proforma.
12. Search `FACT-<year>-000001` and show the unpaid overdue invoice.
13. Search `FACT-<year>-000002` and show the partial invoice: 1,000 RON total, 400 RON collected, 600 RON remaining.
14. Search `CH-2026-001`.
15. Search `OP-DEMO-001`.
16. Open the print route for a billing document.
17. Switch from document view to attachment view.
18. Use browser print preview.
19. Show the month registry and CSV export.
20. Log in with `receptie@demo.local` and show permission-filtered navigation.
21. Log in with `tehnician1@demo.local` and show restricted navigation.
22. Log in with `curier@demo.local` and show courier navigation.
23. Verify `/health`.

All people, clinics, fiscal identifiers and payments in the demo are fictive. Payment rows are manual evidence only; the application does not process money.

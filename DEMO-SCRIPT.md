# Demo script

1. Open `/login` and use “Acces rapid pentru demonstrație” → “Intră ca manager” when demo mode is enabled.
2. Open Panou principal and show the authenticated shell.
3. Open Lucrări.
4. Search `Maria Dumitrescu`.
5. Filter by `Clinica Dentară Aurora`.
6. Filter by `Dr. Ana Popescu`.
7. Open work `WO-2026-900001` and show “Detalii specifice lucrării” with the immutable saved form snapshot.
8. Create a new “Coroană zirconiu” work and show dynamic fields: Dinți, Nuanță, Tip zirconiu, Probă solicitată and Observații specifice.
9. Open Tipuri de lucrări, open “Coroană zirconiu”, then open “Configurează fluxul”.
10. Show workflow versions, linear stages, allowed roles, estimated durations and read-only active template behavior.
11. Open a work and show the QR panel.
12. Open Billing.
13. Select the current month.
14. Show uninvoiced works.
15. Search `PF-<year>-000001` and show the issued proforma.
16. Search `FACT-<year>-000001` and show the unpaid overdue invoice.
17. Search `FACT-<year>-000002` and show the partial invoice: 1,000 RON total, 400 RON collected, 600 RON remaining.
18. Search `CH-2026-001`.
19. Search `OP-DEMO-001`.
20. Open the print route for a billing document.
21. Switch from document view to attachment view.
22. Use browser print preview.
23. Show the month registry and CSV export.
24. Use demo quick access to enter as Recepție and show permission-filtered navigation.
25. Use demo quick access to enter as Tehnician and show restricted navigation.
26. Use demo quick access to enter as Curier and show courier navigation.
27. Verify `/health`.

All people, clinics, fiscal identifiers and payments in the demo are fictive. Payment rows are manual evidence only; the application does not process money.
